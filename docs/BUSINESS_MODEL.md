### Stream 3: Patient Booking Fee (10% of Revenue)

**Model:** Patients pay a small convenience fee for online booking

| Booking Type | Fee | Who Pays | Revenue Share |
|--------------|-----|----------|---------------|
| Free slots | ₹0 | N/A | N/A |
| Priority slots | ₹20-50 | Patient | 100% to us |
| Same-day booking | ₹30-50 | Patient | 100% to us |
| After-hours booking | ₹50-100 | Patient | 50% doctor, 50% us |

**Why It Works:**
- Patients already pay ₹50-100 platform fee on Practo
- Doctor gets patient without commission
- We monetize demand, not supply

### Stream 4: Optional Cloud Backup (5% of Revenue)

For doctors who want off-site backup (without compromising offline-first):

| Plan | Storage | Price |
|------|---------|-------|
| Basic | 5 GB | ₹99/month |
| Standard | 25 GB | ₹249/month |

**Privacy-First Approach:**
- End-to-end encrypted
- Doctor holds encryption keys
- We cannot read the data
- Optional, not required

## Why This Model Works

### 1. Low Price ≠ Low Value
- Offline-first is technically harder, not cheaper
- Voice agent is premium feature, included in ₹999
- We're not racing to bottom, we're disrupting pricing model

### 2. Volume Over Margin
- India has 1.2M+ doctors
- Even 1% = 12,000 customers
- Low price = faster adoption = network effects

### 3. Ecosystem Lock-In (Positive)
- EMR + Practice Manager = complete solution
- Switching cost increases with data
- But we never hold data hostage (export always free)

### 4. SMS/WhatsApp is Recurring
- Once doctors see reduced no-shows, they keep buying
- High margin, usage-based revenue
- Scales with practice growth

## Pricing Principles (Never Violate)

1. **Zero commission. Forever.** This is our core promise.
2. **Free tier must be genuinely useful.** Not crippled.
3. **No hidden fees.** Price is price.
4. **Annual discount is real.** Save 15-20%.
5. **Export is always free.** No data hostage.
6. **Downgrade anytime.** No lock-in tricks.

## Go-To-Market Pricing Strategy

### Phase 1: Early Adopters (First 1,000)
- 50% lifetime discount for first 500 paid users
- Free Professional tier for 3 months
- Goal: Build testimonials and case studies

### Phase 2: Growth (1,000 → 10,000)
- Standard pricing
- Referral program: 1 month free for each referral
- IMA chapter partnerships

### Phase 3: Scale (10,000+)
- Enterprise tier for hospital chains
- API access for integrators
- White-label licensing
